Two guided AR lessons

Capture the physical device you own as the tracking marker, and follow the step-by-step guides overlaid in augmented reality.

Hardware

Motherboard assembly

Capture your motherboard, then place CPU, RAM, power, and case components on the exact hotspots overlaid on your board.

Network

RJ45 crimping & wiring

Capture an RJ45 port, choose straight-through or crossover, and practice stripping, ordering wires, inserting, and crimping both ends.
